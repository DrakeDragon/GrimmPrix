# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- The Gingerbread Man is now called **Gingerbread Man** everywhere.
- Baba Yaga has been removed from the roster and from all text.
- **10 new racers** with distinct stats: Frog Prince, Papa Bear, Dragon, Sly Fox, Mermaid, Pinocchio, Bridge Troll, Genie, Puss in Boots and Rumpel (Rumpelstiltskin). The roster is now 15.
- Each race uses you plus 5 opponents chosen at random from the other 14. A cup keeps the same 5 opponents for its four races, and the Daily Challenge uses the same opponents for everyone on a given date.
- 40+ new Cheshire Cat lines for the new racers (introductions and side-by-side banter).
- Saved racer choices that referred to a removed racer fall back to the Gingerbread Man.

## 1.0.0

First public release.

**Racing**
- Pseudo-3D kart racer, 15 racers (6 per race), 3 laps, 6 items, boost pads, item boxes, drifting with mini-turbo boosts.
- One-thumb slide steering with auto-drift and steering assist, plus a classic buttons scheme and keyboard support.
- Competition setting (Relaxed, Normal, Racer) with gentle rubber-banding.

**Content**
- 16 tracks in 4 cups: Grimm, Wicked, Storybook and Enchanted. Later cups unlock by finishing the one before.
- Track mechanics: wind gusts, a midnight day-to-night shift, mud and sand patches, slippery ice, permanent road hazards (thorns, rats, fire, crabs, scorpions, rocks), jump pads.
- Cheshire Cat announcer with 400+ lines, shuffled so they do not repeat back to back, plus optional spoken narration.
- Procedural synth music that changes per cup and track.

**Modes and progression**
- Cup, Quick Race, Time Trial with a ghost of your best run, 2-player pass-the-phone Duel, Daily Challenge with streaks, Mirror Mode.
- Coins, a Garage with 9 kart paints, a Workshop with 3 upgrades, 18 trophies, stats.
- Shareable ghosts: send a link, a friend imports it and races your run.

**Technical**
- One HTML file, no dependencies, no build step, works offline once loaded.
